## Overview

Designed for those new to digital mapping tools *and* for users familiar with ArcGIS Pro who are interested in learning QGIS, this workshop offers a guided introduction to **QGIS**—a free, open-source geographic information system (GIS).

QGIS is widely used by governments, researchers, and nonprofit organizations to visualize, analyze, and communicate spatial data in ways that tables alone cannot. Participants will gain hands-on experience navigating the QGIS interface and working with spatial data to create meaningful maps.
