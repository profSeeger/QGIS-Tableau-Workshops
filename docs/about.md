**Instructors**
The workshops will be taught by **Professor Christopher J. Seeger, GISP, PLA**  and Extension Specialist Jay Maxwell.

![Professor Christopher J. Seeger](/assets/images/cjseeger_0.jpg){:width="120px" style="float:left; margin-right:15px;"}

Professor Seeger is a Morrill Professor and Extension Specialist in the Department of Landscape Architecture at **Iowa State University**, where his work focuses on **data crowdsourcing, geospatial technology, data visualization, and community-driven mapping and planning**. He has led extension and outreach programs in spatial data and geospatial tools for decades and has trained hundreds of learners through in‑person and online workshops and courses.

He teaches courses on **web mapping, spatial data visualization, and GIS**, covering modern tools such as **QGIS, R, and Tableau**.

Seeger’s research and application interests include integrating geospatial technologies with collaborative design processes and public participation GIS, developing accessible community indicators and datasets, and applying spatial data science to real-world challenges. He leads the ISU Extension and Outreach Indicators Program and co‑directs the Data Science for the Public Good Young Scholars program.

He holds a Master of Landscape Architecture from Iowa State University and is a **Certified Geographic Information Systems Professional (GISP)** and a **Profesional  Landscape Architect**.

Maxwell is an Extension Specialist with Iowa State University Extensiona nd Outreach's Communities and Economic Development unit. In his role as a Data Analyst, Maxwell  provides expertise in Data analysis using R and GIS to solve complex community problems through data. Maxwell teaches workshops in R and ArcGIS Pro and and is a co-author of Extension's *Data for Decision Makers* publications.
