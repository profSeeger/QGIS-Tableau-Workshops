## About These Workshops

Welcome! I'm passionate about making geospatial analysis and data visualization accessible to everyone. These workshops grew from years of teaching colleagues, students, and community members how to leverage powerful open-source and industry-standard tools to work with data more effectively.

### Workshop Philosophy

My teaching approach emphasizes:
- **Hands-on learning** - You learn by doing, not just watching
- **Real-world applications** - All exercises use realistic scenarios and datasets
- **Best practices** - Not just how to use the tools, but how to use them well
- **Open resources** - Preference for open-source tools and freely available data
- **Continued support** - Learning doesn't end when the workshop does

### What Makes These Workshops Different

- **Small class sizes** - Maximum 25 participants for personalized attention
- **All materials provided** - Download data, slides, and reference guides
- **Beginner-friendly** - No previous experience required for intro workshops
- **Installation support** - Help available before and during workshops
- **Take-home resources** - Keep all materials for future reference
- **Community connection** - Join a network of learners

### Technologies I Teach

**Current Workshop Series:**
- **QGIS** - Open-source GIS for mapping and spatial analysis
- **Tableau** - Interactive data visualization and dashboards

**Expanding Soon:**
- Python for geospatial analysis
- Web mapping with Leaflet
- R for spatial statistics
- ArcGIS Pro essentials

### Who Should Attend

These workshops are designed for:
- Researchers analyzing spatial data
- Urban planners and environmental professionals
- Data analysts and business intelligence practitioners
- Students building technical skills
- Non-profit staff working with community data
- Anyone curious about maps and data visualization!

No programming experience required for beginner workshops.

### Workshop Format

**Typical Structure:**
- Full-day workshops (9 AM - 4 PM)
- Morning: Fundamentals and guided exercises
- Afternoon: Applied projects and advanced topics
- Breaks and lunch provided
- Hands-on lab computers available (or bring your own laptop)
